07

Frequently asked questions

What photo do I need to send?

One clear, front-facing photo — a phone photo works. If your package includes a scene, you can also send a photo of your shop, your truck or your stage.

Exactly what files do I receive?

Depending on your package: transparent PNG, high-resolution JPG and a print-ready PDF. Everything is delivered digitally by email — no shipping waits.

Can I use my character in my business?

Yes. Every package includes a commercial license: you can use your character on social media, cards, your shop, your menu and your sales material.

How long does my order take?

Standard delivery is 7 days per the production calendar. The Premium package skips 2 days of queue, and a 72-hour express (+$25) is available subject to capacity.

What if I want another product later?

Your character stays on file at the studio: every new ladder product is made from your original order's files, without starting from scratch.

Are the characters on this page real clients?

No. They are fictional characters created by the studio as a sample of each trade. Yours is created from your photo.
